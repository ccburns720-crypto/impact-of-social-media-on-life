Impact of Social Media on Life

A Data-Driven Story on Student Performance and Mental Well-being

About This Project

How does our digital footprint affect our daily lives? This interactive dashboard explores a dataset containing records of student screen time, academic grades, platform choices, mental health indicators, and sleep habits. Scroll down to interact with the visualizations.

1. Screen Time & Grades

Takeaway: This scatter plot shows that higher daily social media usage has a slight downward correlation with student GPAs.

2. Preferred Platforms by Demographics

Takeaway: This grouped bar chart reveals how younger students lean towards platforms like TikTok, while older students prefer Instagram or YouTube.

3. Well-being Analysis

Takeaway: This boxplot shows that students reporting 'High' anxiety or depression levels tend to have a higher median daily screen time.

4. Sleep Quality Distribution

Takeaway: The density heatmap highlights concentration clusters showing the relationship between specific app usage and self-reported sleep quality categories.
